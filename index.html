About the studio

Welcome to the workshop.

We’re Shiny Rock Games, an indie game studio building weird, fun and slightly chaotic games. We like strange ideas, unexpected mechanics and making things that are just a little bit different.

From chaotic tiddlywinks in Scrunge to whatever we dream up next, there’s always something brewing in the workshop.

Come see what we’re making. Things might get weird.

Meet the team

Three founders, one shared studio, each leading our own corner.

Nieve

Business and web lead

Nieve keeps the studio running and the website shining. She co-programs our games, runs our marketing and socials, and by day manages a busy online retailer's website. She also built and launched Orbit Studios, a CRM web app for small creative studios and freelancers.

Gavin

Audio and art lead

Gavin works as Von Boom, bringing over 30 years of music, design and visual arts to the table, with music and voice acting credits that include the BBC's The Golden Cobra. He composes the Scrunge soundtrack, voices our videos, and keeps everyone's spirits (and momentum) high.

James

Lead developer and production

James has built most of Scrunge and gave it its hand-made pog look. A published Unity developer, he released Creature Castle on Google Play and handled everything from testing to launch. He also runs our Steam page and captures all the gameplay footage.
