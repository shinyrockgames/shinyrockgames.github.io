Scrunge is a chaotic tiddlywinks roguelike.

Follow on TikTok Demo news on Discord

How it plays

01

Flick

Flick your wink across the mat, line up your shots and send it flying into the chaos. Master your aim, find the perfect angle and make every flick count.

02

Smash

Bounce off obstacles, smash into things and rack up your multiplier. The bigger the combo, the bigger the score, so keep the momentum going!

03

Pot

Sink your wink to complete the round, bank your score and unlock what comes next. Build your run, discover new possibilities and see just how far you can push it.

Collect the pogs

Every perk in Scrunge is a pog. Stack them, swap them, and bend the rules of the table in your favour.
